D · META-ANALYSIS CALCULATOR

Pool odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals

Statistical demo

Enter values copied and checked from original studies. This calculator demonstrates fixed-effect and DerSimonian–Laird random-effects calculations. It does not decide whether studies are scientifically comparable.

y = ln(OR) · SE = [ln(upper CI) − ln(lower CI)] / 3.92 · weight = 1 / SE²
Enter at least two studies. Use only verified OR and 95% CI values.
Interpretation boundaryDo not pool studies merely because they report an OR. Check population, outcome definitions, effect measures, study design, and risk of bias. A pooled association is not proof of causation.

What is childhood-onset lupus?

Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) is an autoimmune condition. In childhood-onset SLE, the disease begins before age 18. It can affect different body systems, and experiences vary from one person to another.

RememberSymptoms and test results need to be assessed by a qualified clinician. This website cannot tell whether a child has lupus.

About Lupuscope

From evidence to insight — a student research prototype for organizing published research on childhood-onset systemic lupus erythematosus.

Student research prototype
PURPOSE

What Lupuscope is designed to do

Lupuscope aims to organize published cSLE studies, compare findings, and help identify areas of the literature that may need further investigation.

Its current library-level analysis uses study metadata and topic tags. It does not analyze patient-level records.

SOURCES

Where records come from

The starter library contains PubMed / PubMed Central records and official health-information links. The 14 additions are candidate records for the next screening round.

Open the original article and verify each detail before citing it or adding it to the final evidence set.

PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

What this prototype does not provide

  • No secure server-side authentication or verified professional credentials.
  • No encrypted patient database or secure medical-record storage.
  • No diagnosis, disease prediction, treatment recommendation, or clinical decision support.
  • No real clinician moderation of community posts.
  • No clinically validated AI. The default assistant is a guided local demo.
  • Machine translation and automatically generated summaries may contain errors and require human review.
Data protectionDo not enter real patient information. Local browser storage is not a secure medical-data environment. A public launch would require specialist, security, privacy, and legal review.
